Privacy Policy — TV Remote

Last updated: 2026-11-13 · Contact: fonixysllc@gmail.com

What the app does on your network. To find and control your TV, the app sends discovery messages on your local Wi-Fi and talks directly to your TV. TV names, IP addresses, pairing keys and tokens are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

Data we do not collect. No account, no name, no email, no contacts, no location. The app does not request microphone or camera permission.

Voice commands. When you tap the microphone button, your phone's own speech recognition service (provided by Google on most Android phones) opens, listens and converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. Our app does not have microphone access, never receives the audio, and only gets the recognized text, which it turns into a remote-control command and then discards.

Advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. They may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google UMP); you can change your choice at any time. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics. The app records anonymous usage events (for example "TV connected", "paywall shown") to improve reliability. They are not linked to your identity.

Model reports. If you choose "Report my model", an email draft opens in your own mail app with your TV model and basic diagnostics. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the device. For any request, write to the contact address above.
