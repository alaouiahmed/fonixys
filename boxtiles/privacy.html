Privacy Policy — Boxtiles

Last updated: 2026-11-13 · Contact: fonixysllc@gmail.com

Boxtiles (com.fonixys.boxtiles.remote) is a Wi-Fi remote control for IPTV boxes running Android, published by Fonixys. It is a remote control only: it provides no channels or content.

What the app does on your network. To find and control your boxes, the app sends discovery messages on your local Wi-Fi and talks directly to each box. Box names, the room names you choose, IP addresses, tile layouts, favourite apps and pairing keys are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

Data we do not collect. No account, no name, no email, no contacts, no location. The app does not request microphone or camera permission.

Voice commands. When you tap the voice tile, your phone's own speech recognition service (provided by Google on most Android phones) opens, listens and converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. Boxtiles has no microphone access, never receives the audio, and only gets the recognized text, which it turns into a remote-control command and then discards.

Advertising. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. They may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings ("Ad privacy choices"). See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics and crash reports. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record usage events (for example "box connected", "ad watched") with an app-instance ID, and Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (device model, Android version, app state at the time of the crash). They help us fix problems and improve the app, are not linked to your identity, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland follow the consent choice you made in the app. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Model reports. If you choose "Report my box model", an email draft opens in your own mail app with your box brand and model and basic diagnostics. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the device. For any request, write to the contact address above.
