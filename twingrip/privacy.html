Privacy Policy — Twingrip

Last updated: 2026-11-13 · Contact: fonixysllc@gmail.com

Twingrip ("the app") is a Wi-Fi remote control for IPTV boxes running Android, published by Fonixys. It is a remote control only: it provides no channels or content.

What the app does on your network. To find and control your box, the app sends discovery messages on your local Wi-Fi and talks directly to the box. Box names, IP addresses, pairing keys and tokens are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Remote-control commands go straight from your phone to your box over your local network.

Data we do not collect. No account, no name, no email, no contacts, no location. The app does not request microphone or camera permission.

Voice commands. When you tap Voice, your phone's own speech recognition service (provided by your phone's maker or by Google on most Android phones) opens, listens and converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. Twingrip has no microphone access, never receives the audio, and only gets the recognized text, which it turns into a remote-control command and then discards.

Advertising. The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. They may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Premium features unlock after a short, optional rewarded ad; there is nothing to buy.

Analytics and crash reports. If we enable Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics in a later version, they will record usage events (for example "box connected", "ad watched") with an app-instance ID, and crash reports (device model, Android version, app state at the time of the crash). They help us fix problems, are not linked to your identity, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland follow the consent choice you made in the app. This policy will be updated before that happens. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Model reports. If you choose "Report my model", an email draft opens in your own mail app with your box model and basic diagnostics. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the device. For any request, write to fonixysllc@gmail.com.
