Privacy Policy — Sayline

Last updated: 2026-11-13 · Contact: fonixysllc@gmail.com

Sayline ("the app") is a remote control for IPTV boxes running Android TV, published by Fonixys. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What the app does on your network. To find and control your box, the app sends discovery messages on your local Wi-Fi and talks directly to the box. Box names, IP addresses, pairing keys and tokens are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Remote commands go straight from your phone to the box over your local network.

Data we do not collect. No account, no name, no email, no contacts, no location. The app does not request microphone or camera permission.

Voice commands. When you tap the microphone button, your phone's own speech recognition service (provided by Google on most Android phones) opens, listens and converts your speech to text under its own privacy policy. Sayline does not have microphone access, never receives the audio, and only gets the recognized text, which it turns into a remote-control command.

Typed commands and command buttons. Text you type in the command bar is sent to your box over your local network (as a command, or typed into the box's text field). The commands you use are kept on your phone as one-tap buttons; you can delete them at any time in the settings. They are never sent to us.

Advertising. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners. They may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy choices" in the settings. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics and crash reports. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record usage events (for example "box connected", "ad watched") with an app-instance ID, and Firebase Crashlytics to send crash reports (device model, Android version, app state at the time of the crash). These events never contain what you say or type. They help us fix problems and improve the app, are not linked to your identity, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland follow the consent choice you made in the app. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Model reports. If you choose "Report my box model", an email draft opens in your own mail app with your box model and basic diagnostics. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the device. For any request, write to the contact address above.
